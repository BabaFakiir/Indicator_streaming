Now, listen to me carefully. I want a dynamic system for monitoring Banknifty option ticks.

Since, we do not know what our strike value for the day will be, we take +-1000 buffer for the stream. For eg if we close the day at BANKNIFTY 61200.00, we monitor options 
BANKNIFTY 60200 - 62200 PE and CE for the nearest expiry. Also note that Banknifty options have monthly expiry at last tuesday of every month. Just like this month is 24 Feb. 

Now, at 9:00 am IST, we check the last LTP of Banknifty, decide what options to monitor for today based on +-1000 point buffer and then find their symbol tokens. 
symbol tokens for Angelone are present in https://margincalculator.angelbroking.com/OpenAPI_File/files/OpenAPIScripMaster.json .
